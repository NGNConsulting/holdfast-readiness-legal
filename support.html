Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? Only on your phone, encrypted. There is no account and no cloud copy.
  • How do I keep a backup? In Setup, tap "Back up now", choose a passphrase, and save the file somewhere safe, such as a drive or another device. Keep the passphrase separately.
  • I forgot my backup passphrase. It cannot be reset or recovered, by you or by us. That is what keeps the file private.
  • How do I move to a new phone? Make a backup on the old phone, copy the file to the new one, then use "Restore from backup" in Setup.
  • How do I share a cache with family? Open the cache and tap "Share this cache". Choose what to include, then send the passphrase separately from the file.
  • Does scanning send my photos anywhere? No. Photos are read on your phone and deleted afterward. Typing details in is always available as the fully private option.
  • I forgot my Defense and Valuables PIN. It is separate from your phone's PIN and fingerprint, and nobody can recover it, including us. On the locked screen, tap "Forgot PIN": this permanently deletes your Defense and Valuables entries on that phone and lets you set a new PIN. Everything else stays. If you have an encrypted backup, you can restore it afterwards.
  • How do I delete everything? Setup has "Erase all data". Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Why is a best-by date marked "Estimated"? It was worked out from a manufacturer code, or from the purchase date, plus a typical shelf life. Check the can itself before relying on it.
