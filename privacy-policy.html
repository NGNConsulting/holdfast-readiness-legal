Holdfast Readiness

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Holdfast Readiness ("the app") is a household stockpile tracker published by NGN Consulting Corp. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app, and our website for it.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We never receive it and cannot see, recover or sell it.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to, for a few optional features described below, each with a notice in the app.
  • If you email us, we receive what you send, and we use it only to reply.

Who we are

NGN Consulting Corp. is a company based in Illinois, USA. For the limited personal data we do receive, described under "Information we receive", we are the controller. You can reach us at ngn.consulting.corp@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

What you enter in the app stays in a database on your phone. That includes your supplies, food and nutrition details, medicines, gear, any Defense or Valuables records, caches with their descriptions and coordinates, and your settings. Some of it, such as a list of medicines, may reveal information about your health.

The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (256-bit AES). The encryption key is generated on your phone and kept in its secure key storage (the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain). It is not included in cloud backups of your phone, and the app turns off Android cloud backup for its own data.

We have no copy of your data or your key. That protects your privacy, but it also means we cannot recover your data if your phone is lost or the key is lost. You can delete everything at any time with "Erase all data" in Setup, or by uninstalling the app.

Backups and shared files

The app creates a backup file or a shared cache file only when you ask it to. Each file is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and you decide where it goes, using your phone's own sharing tools. Anyone who has both the file and its passphrase can read it. We never receive these files, and a file you have sent cannot be recalled.

Optional features and who they involve

None of these features is needed to use the app. Typing information in yourself keeps everything on your phone.

  • Scanning (camera and photos). When you choose to scan a nutrition label, a barcode or a code on a can, the image is read on your phone using Google ML Kit, and the photo is deleted once it has been read. On Android, the app removes ML Kit's usage reporting, so nothing is sent to Google. On iPhone, Google may receive your IP address with device and usage statistics, as described in Google's ML Kit terms. The app explains this and asks before scanning is first used, and you can turn scanning off in Setup.
  • Online barcode lookup. Only when you tap "Look up online", the app sends the barcode number, and nothing else, to Open Food Facts, a non-profit based in France, which returns product information. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your IP address. See its privacy policy at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • Current location. Only when you tap "Use my current location" while editing a cache, the app asks your phone's location service for your position and saves the coordinates on your phone only. On Android this service is part of Google Play services. If "Google Location Accuracy" is turned on in your phone's settings, Google may process location data under its own privacy policy. Typing coordinates in avoids this.
  • Open in Maps. If you choose to open a cache's coordinates in your maps app, they are handed to that app, and its provider handles them under its own policy.
  • App lock. If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Links. The More Apps list opens Google Play, and Send Feedback opens your email app. What happens there is governed by those services.

Information we receive

  • Emails you send us. If you write to ngn.consulting.corp@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to reply and to fix problems you report. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which stores messages on our behalf. We keep messages until the matter is resolved and delete them within 24 months, unless we need them longer for a legal reason. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • App store statistics. Google Play and the Apple App Store give us statistics such as install counts, ratings, and crash and performance reports from people who chose to share diagnostics with Google or Apple. These are summarized and are not linked to your identity. Google and Apple collect them under their own privacy policies.
  • Purchases. If the app offers an optional purchase, it is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not sell or "share" personal information (as those words are used in California law), show ads, build profiles, track you across apps or websites, or use any analytics service. The app has no user accounts.

Our website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations under its own privacy statement. Our pages set no cookies, run no analytics, and load no fonts, scripts or images from other websites.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Information on your phone is processed by the app on your device, for you. We do not receive it. For emails you send us, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the app. Optional features run only at your request, and where consent is required we ask for it in the app first. You can withdraw it at any time, for example by turning off scanning in Setup.

International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you email us from outside the US, your message is processed in the US by us and by our email provider, Google. Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

Your rights

Because your app data never leaves your phone, you control it directly: you can view, change, export it as a backup, or erase it at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights over any personal data we hold about you, such as emails. These can include rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of it, and to object to or restrict its use. If you are in the EEA or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority. California and other US state residents have rights to know, correct and delete, and to not be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information or use sensitive personal information.

To exercise any right, email ngn.consulting.corp@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK, and we do not knowingly receive their personal data.

Security

The app encrypts your data on your phone and in backup files, and can lock itself with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone, its screen lock and your backup passphrases safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, the app will show you the updated policy and ask you to accept it before you continue.
